📜 Official Donk Pro Tournament Rules

The following regulations apply to head-to-head singles and doubles match play.

🎯 Match Objective

  • First player or team to reach 21 points wins the match.
  • Cancellation Scoring applies at the end of each round.
  • Skunk Rule: A score of 13–0 ends the match immediately.

⚖️ Referee & Opening

  • Referee: Neutral third party keeps score, awards Style Bonus, and settles disputes. Referee decisions are final.
  • Opening Ritual: DONKs kiss (tap snouts) before match start.
  • Leadoff Throw: Selected by referee for round 1. Subsequent first throws go to the player who scored last.

🎯 Legal Toss Regulations

  • Must throw from behind and within the marked 5 ft toss line.
  • Minimum of 1 full rotation required in mid-air.
  • Do NOT hold the DONK by its tail or ears during throw.
  • DONK must touch the scoring mat. Illegal toss = 0 points.

⚡ Scoring Modifiers & Leaners

  • Center Spot: Touching center hex = 2× score multiplier.
  • Style Bonus: +1 point per toss at referee's discretion.
  • Leaner: A DONK leaning against an opponent's scoring DONK steals all its points.

💥 Knock-Out & Defense

  • Second tosser may collide with opponent's DONK on the mat.
  • Attacking DONK must land in a scoring position.
  • If attacker fails to score, opponent keeps higher of original or final position score.

👑 Farewell Toss Counter

  • Triggered immediately when an Immaculate DONK is landed.
  • Opponent receives 1 response toss. Must land an Immaculate DONK or Leaner to stay alive.
  • No Knock-Outs allowed during Farewell Toss.

⚙️ Equipment Setup & Inflation Guidelines

📦 Package Contents

  • 2 Official DONKs (Inflatable competition targets)
  • 1 DONK Pro Scoring Mat
  • 1 Donkflator Air Pump

🌬️ Proper Inflation (0.5 PSI)

  • Inflate using the included Donkflator Pump.
  • Target pressure is approx 0.5 PSI. When the snout feels firm, your DONK Pro is at proper PSI.
  • Do NOT over-inflate: If your DONK's waist starts swelling, it has received too much air.
  • Ensure both DONKs on the court have the exact same level of inflation for fair play.

📐 Court & Toss Line Layout

  • Lay out the DONK Pro Scoring Mat flat on a level surface.
  • Mark a 5 ft wide Toss Line positioned exactly 5 feet from the front point of the scoring mat.
